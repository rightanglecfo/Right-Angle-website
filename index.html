Business Intelligence

Automated financial & operational reporting.

  • KPI reporting & dashboardingDefining the metrics that matter and automating dashboards that leadership can act on and trust. AI-powered solutions and modern tools (e.g., Tableau, Power BI, API-connected automations).
  • BI reporting & data visualizationTurning transactional data into clear, decision-ready reporting.

Financial Planning & Analysis

Understanding actual financial results and forward-looking insights.

  • Scenario & sensitivity modelingStress-testing growth, financing, and cost assumptions, accelerated with AI-assisted modeling tools.

  • 13-week cash flow forecastingRolling short-term visibility for lenders, investors, and internal decisions.
