Services Offered

Custom solutions for the finance & accounting needs of your business.

Business Intelligence

Automated financial & operational reporting.

  • KPI reporting & dashboardingDefining the metrics that matter and automating dashboards that leadership can act on and trust. AI-powered solutions and modern tools (e.g., Tableau, Power BI, API-connected automations).
  • BI reporting & data visualizationTurning transactional data into clear, decision-ready reporting.
  • AI-driven reporting automationAI-powered workflows that pull, clean, and refresh data without manual rework. Modern reporting and automation solutions (e.g., Power BI, Tableau, API-connections).
  • ERP & systems integrationConnecting ERP, CRM, and other business systems into a single automated reporting layer.

Financial Planning & Analysis

Understanding actual financial results and forward-looking insights.

  • Three-statement modelingIntegrated income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow forecasts built to withstand diligence.
  • Scenario & sensitivity modelingStress-testing growth, financing, and cost assumptions, accelerated with AI-assisted modeling tools.
  • Long-range planningMulti-year plans that tie strategy to the numbers behind it.
  • 13-week cash flow forecastingRolling short-term visibility for lenders, investors, and internal decisions.

Other Office of the CFO Services

  • Close process automationAI-assisted month-end close with fewer manual touchpoints and faster turnaround.
  • Lender & investor-ready reportingFinancials and packages built to hold up under bank, PE, or VC scrutiny.

  • Interim finance leadershipExperienced financial leadership support through a transaction, turn-around, or scale-up.
